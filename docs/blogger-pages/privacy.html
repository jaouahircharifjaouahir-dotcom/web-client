Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the YouTube Thumbnail Extractor at https://www.11tik.com/.

Processing in the browser

When you paste a YouTube URL, the page reads the video ID and checks public thumbnail image URLs in your browser. Those pasted links are not stored on an 11tik application server. This product does not have a backend that stores video URLs.

Local history

The extractor may save recent URLs in this browser’s local storage so you can open them again. That copy stays on your device. Clear it with the in-page history control, or by clearing this site’s data in the browser. It is not a cloud account.

Hosting logs

The website and its static files are served by the hosting providers used for 11tik.com. Those hosts can keep ordinary web logs such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, according to their own policies.

Third parties

Thumbnail files are requested from YouTube’s public image hosts. The site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-DT1T9J27G5) to count visits and in-page actions such as extract and download. Google may set cookies or similar identifiers. Pasted YouTube URLs are not sent as analytics event parameters. Fonts or scripts may also load from CDNs used to display the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the Contact form. Messages are emailed to the site operator. The form delivery service can see the name, email, and message you submit.
