Batch Download YouTube Thumbnails for Research

When you audit a niche, you need many stills fast. 11tik Bulk mode accepts one URL per line, shows each video’s best public thumbnail, and can zip the highest-quality set.

Workflow

  1. Open 11tik and enable Bulk.
  2. Paste up to 25 public YouTube or Vimeo links.
  3. Extract, then download individually or as a zip of best stills.

Use results for moodboards and competitive research. Do not republish others’ art without rights.
