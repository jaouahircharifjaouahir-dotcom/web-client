About 11tik

Last updated: 22 August 2026

11tik is the publisher of the YouTube Thumbnail Extractor at www.11tik.com. The site is run for people who need the public preview image of a YouTube video without installing software or uploading the link to a processing server.

Who publishes this site?

Content and the extractor are published under the 11tik name. There is no invented expert persona and no fabricated review scores. Editorial pages explain how public thumbnail files work; they do not claim to be YouTube or Google.

What does 11tik do?

The tool reads a public video ID in the browser, checks which thumbnail files exist on YouTube image hosts, and lets you download or copy those stills. Parsing and ranking stay on your device. You can work with one URL, a list of URLs, or a public channel feed of recent uploads.

What does 11tik not do?

  • It does not download YouTube video or audio.
  • It does not bypass private, age-gated, or region-blocked videos.
  • It does not require an account.
  • It does not claim copyright in anyone else’s thumbnail.

Language versions

The extractor UI is available on language hosts such as fr.11tik.com and ar.11tik.com. These legal pages stay on www.11tik.com so there is one canonical About, Privacy, Contact, and Terms URL.
