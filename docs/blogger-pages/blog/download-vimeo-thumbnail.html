How to Download a Vimeo Thumbnail Without a Screenshot

Vimeo serves thumbnail images through its oEmbed API. 11tik reads that public response in your browser, validates larger CDN variants when they exist, then lets you download or copy the image URL.

Steps

  1. Copy a public vimeo.com link.
  2. Paste it into 11tik.
  3. Preview available sizes and download.

Private or password videos without public oEmbed data cannot be forced.
