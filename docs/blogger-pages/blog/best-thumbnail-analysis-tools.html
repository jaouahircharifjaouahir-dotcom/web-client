Best free tools to analyze YouTube thumbnails

By 11tik

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Creators compare thumbnails to improve packaging, not to copy art. A solid stack covers extraction of public stills, side-by-side review, and CTR experiments inside YouTube Studio when you are eligible.

Which free tool extracts the public still?

11tik downloads the highest available public still from YouTube in the browser, including batch mode. It does not score CTR and it does not design new art.

Where do I A/B test thumbnails?

YouTube Studio, when the feature is available on the channel. Pair Studio results with saved reference stills. Respect copyright when you study competitors.

What should I use to design originals?

Figma, Photopea, or similar editors. Start from your footage or licensed assets, then confirm the published file with 11tik after upload.
