Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy applies to the YouTube Thumbnail Extractor at https://www.11tik.com/, the language hosts that load the same extractor, and the 11tik browser extension.

Processing in the browser

When you paste a YouTube URL, the page reads the video ID and checks public thumbnail image URLs in your browser. Those pasted links are not stored as a personal account on an 11tik application server. Extraction does not require a login.

Optional share-page index

After a successful extract, the browser may send only a public video ID, title, tags derived from public metadata, and a public thumbnail URL to our Cloudflare Worker so the video can appear on a share page and, if it passes a quality check, in the sitemap. We do not ask you to upload the image file. You can use the extractor without that index if the request is blocked by your browser.

Local history

The extractor may save recent URLs in this browser’s local storage so you can open them again. That copy stays on your device. Clear it with the in-page history control, or by clearing this site’s data in the browser. It is not a cloud account.

Hosting logs

The website, language hosts, and static files are served by the hosting providers used for 11tik.com (including Cloudflare and GitHub Pages). Those hosts can keep ordinary web logs such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, according to their own policies.

Third parties

Thumbnail files are requested from YouTube's public image hosts. The site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-FW7B8NDZZ5) to count visits and in-page actions such as extract and download. Google may set cookies or similar identifiers. Pasted YouTube URLs are not sent as analytics event parameters. Fonts or scripts may also load from CDNs used to display the page.

The contact form is delivered by FormSubmit to the operator’s email. FormSubmit can see the name, email, and message you submit.

11tik Browser Extension

This section describes the 11tik — YouTube Thumbnail Extractor browser extension (Chrome and Firefox). It is separate from the website extractor sections above.

When you use the extension. When you click the extension icon, the extension reads the URL of your active browser tab to obtain the YouTube video ID. This happens only when you open the extension popup; the extension does not run continuously in the background or monitor tabs on its own.

Local processing. The extension extracts the YouTube video ID locally and checks public thumbnail image URLs on YouTube’s image hosts (such as i.ytimg.com) from your browser. Thumbnail data is processed in the browser to show a preview and to support download or copy actions.

What is not sent to 11tik during extraction. The extension does not send your tab URL to 11tik servers to perform extraction. It does not upload the thumbnail file to 11tik servers to perform extraction.

Storage, analytics, and accounts. The extension does not use persistent storage (such as extension storage or local storage) to keep a history of your activity. The extension code does not include analytics or telemetry. Extraction through the extension does not require an account or login. These website behaviors described above—Google Analytics 4, local history on the site, and optional share-page indexing—apply to the website, not to the extension during extraction.

User-initiated actions. If you choose Open in 11tik or copy an 11tik share link, you may open a page on https://www.11tik.com/. Visiting that website is then covered by the website sections of this policy (including optional share-page indexing, local history on the site, analytics on the site, and hosting logs). If you choose Open full resolution, the extension opens the selected public thumbnail URL in a new browser tab.

Third parties. Thumbnail requests from the extension go to YouTube’s public image hosts (such as i.ytimg.com). Those hosts operate under their own policies.

Permissions. The extension requests access to your active tab when you invoke it, and permission to reach https://i.ytimg.com/* for public thumbnail files.
