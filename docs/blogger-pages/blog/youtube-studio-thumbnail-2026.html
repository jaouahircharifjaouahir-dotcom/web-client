YouTube Studio custom thumbnails in 2026

By 11tik

Last updated: 21 August 2026

YouTube Studio still lets eligible channels upload a custom thumbnail on a video’s Details page. Layout labels move, but the job is the same: YouTube hosts a new public still. 11tik does not upload that file; it only reads public image URLs after they exist.

How do I check a thumbnail after I change it in Studio?

Wait until YouTube finishes processing, then paste the watch URL into 11tik. If maxres is missing, the extractor shows the next public size. That is YouTube’s publish state, not a Studio bug inside 11tik.

Does 11tik replace YouTube Studio?

No. Studio is where you upload and A/B test when YouTube offers tests. 11tik is for downloading or copying the public stills that already exist.

What should I do when the image looks old?

Caches on image hosts can lag. Recheck later, or copy the URL 11tik confirms exists. Do not assume a 4K master; public maxres is usually 1280×720.
