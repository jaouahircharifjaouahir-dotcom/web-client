About

11tik publishes the YouTube Thumbnail Extractor at https://www.11tik.com/. The tool does one job: take a public YouTube video or Shorts URL and find the highest available public thumbnail image in your browser.

How it works

Parsing, thumbnail checks, ranking, and download all run on your device. 11tik does not operate a video-processing server.

What it does not do

  • It does not download YouTube video or audio.
  • It does not bypass private or restricted videos.
  • It does not require an account.
