# SEO content governance (Phase 17.1)

Before creating a new article or localized page:

1. **Define exact intent** — one primary search job in plain language.
2. **Identify existing owner** — check `docs/keyword-map.md` and `scripts/i18n/anti-cannibalization-contract.mjs`.
3. **Prove gap** — competitor/public research or product capability not covered elsewhere.
4. **Define unique angle** — how this page differs from siblings (not a near-duplicate H1).
5. **Define internal-link destinations** — parent, 2–5 siblings, utility/embed when relevant.
6. **Define external linkability** — what original data or developer utility makes this citable.
7. **Avoid cannibalization** — do not target protected keywords owned by another URL.
8. **No duplicate translation doorway** — locale pages inherit intent, not keyword-stuffed clones.
9. **No fake claims** — no invented stats, 4K, private APIs, or video download promises.

Reject: thin AI pages, mass locale spam, fake statistics, keyword stuffing, doorway `?k=` ranking URLs.
